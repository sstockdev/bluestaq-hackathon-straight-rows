The five words

Normal is each tractor's usual signal strength: its median C/N0 over all three days.
Drop is how far below normal the signal is right now. Normal minus now.
Sky explains is the drop S4 predicts: 2.1 + 18 × S4 once S4 reaches 0.31, otherwise zero.
Unexplained is drop minus what the sky explains. Jamming lives here.
Gap is the average signal minus the weakest satellite. Antenna faults and multipath widen it.
